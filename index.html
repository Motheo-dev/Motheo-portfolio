About Me

I'm Motheo Theo Moroane, a passionate website developer based in Pretoria, South Africa. I've been building websites for the past 3 years, helping local businesses and individuals establish their online presence.

My journey into web development started when I realized how many small businesses in Pretoria needed quality websites but didn't know where to start. I taught myself to code and have been helping local entrepreneurs bring their ideas to life ever since.

I believe that every business deserves a professional online presence, regardless of budget. That's why I focus on creating clean, functional websites that are easy to maintain and actually help grow your business.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me at local tech meetups in Pretoria, contributing to open-source projects, or enjoying a good cup of coffee while learning new technologies.
